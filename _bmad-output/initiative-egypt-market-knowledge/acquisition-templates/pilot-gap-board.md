## Material-field matrix

For the selected arrangement, record `observed`, `conflicting` or `unknown`, with artifact/page/span and review status (`single-review` or `second-reviewed`). Which fields are material is `[HUMAN-REVIEW:NEEDED]` (scholar). Fields: identity; legal parties; cash price; financed principal; contractual total payable; instalment count/dates; mandatory fees; late/default clauses and beneficiary; early settlement; ownership/delivery; annexes; effective period; customer/channel applicability. The approved rule determines material fields. Arithmetic or marketing mechanisms never fill an unobserved clause.
