## Proposed operational intent

The original implementation Intent establishes the public capture foundation. For this operating walkthrough, the proposed outcome is broader: build five scoped provider dossiers that establish the applicable contracting parties, complete agreement versions and material clauses, or preserve explicit unresolved gaps that prevent unsupported answers. This proposal does not change the historical implementation spec or declare any dossier complete.

**Problem:** The dated identity captures and partial terms do not yet establish complete, applicable financing arrangements for all five providers. Different dossiers have different missing evidence: contracts and schedules, the selected contracting entity, dated name/version reconciliation, or a merchant–financier relationship.

**Approach:** Define each arrangement's product, merchant, financier, channel, period and cohort; request the exact missing instruments through an eligible public, source-supplied or permitted supervised route; preserve originals and claim-level provenance; verify completeness and applicability; and retain conflicts and unknowns until supported. Source-delivered documents and manual research are valid acquisition routes subject to their own scope and rights, even when automation cannot obtain the required artifact.

**Outcome:** Each dossier has an applicable, traceable evidence pack or an explicit unresolved evidence record with an owner and next action. Analyst verification, acquisition rights, product reuse rights and scholar-approved interpretation remain separate decisions. Request dispatch or capture success alone does not close a dossier.

This covers valU's agreement/app-role split, Contact's arrangement-specific counterparty, Souhoola's complete text and dated identity conflict, Aman's consumer-finance role, and Halan's missing merchant relationship. Priority remains revisable as actual artifacts and dependencies change.

## Procedure examination: operational refinements

1. **Separate request readiness from document availability.** Current tasks state the missing evidence but do not select product, merchant/channel and period. Each outgoing brief must name these or explicitly ask for scope clarification. An unresolved field is a preparation task, not a completed request.
2. **Separate contact verification from identity evidence.** Historical register contact details help discovery; verify the official receiving channel and its document-owning role before dispatch. A familiar brand domain or shared address does not establish who signs the agreement.
3. **Use a two-stage request where necessary.** Contact and Halan first need arrangement/counterparty evidence. Ask the identified owner for the applicable pack after that relationship is supported. Souhoola needs the complete pack and a separate dated identity clarification; neither substitutes for the other.
4. **Accept clauses and applicability, not a successful download.** Record document ID/version, page or exact span, effective period, annex completeness and arrangement match. Distinguish publication, effective and capture dates. A provider email can clarify routing or scope, but does not replace operative clauses for contractual obligations.
5. **Choose the acquisition route per artifact.** An official supplied file bundle may close a gap without automated scraping. For public collection, a current scoped access decision is still required. Robots, security refusal, authentication, source terms and reuse permission remain separate states. Browser extraction, OCR and private intake must not be reported as implemented automatic capabilities.

### 1. valU — reference contract dossier

**Scope to fill:** [product], [merchant or direct channel], [customer/cohort], [applicable period].

**Request:** Please supply the standard unsigned finance agreement applicable to this scope, including incorporated terms, fee and repayment schedules, early-settlement provisions and late/default provisions. ### 2. Contact — contracting-owner clarification first

**Scope to fill:** [specific merchant/product offer], [channel], [period], [non-personal description of the arrangement].

**First request:** Which legal entity signs or finances this arrangement? Please provide its exact legal name, relevant activity/licence identifier and a dated merchant/provider document naming its role. Please identify the document owner who can supply the applicable standard agreement and schedules.

**Why:** The worksheet records six consumer-finance entities under the brand and a separate factoring entity. Email domains and group branding cannot select the contracting entity.

### 3. Souhoola — complete terms and dated identity reconciliation

**Scope to fill:** [product], [merchant/channel], [period]; identify whether the earlier February 2026 website terms are relevant to that scope.

**Request:** Please supply the complete applicable finance agreement, website terms where incorporated, and all referenced schedules. Separately clarify the relationship among the observed names “CI Consumer Finance Souhoola co.”, the FRA Arabic BM/formerly-CI wording, and “Contact Investment for Consumer Finance” in the earlier partial terms. ### 4. Aman — consumer-finance role and obligations

**Scope to fill:** [consumer-finance product], [merchant/channel], [cohort/period].

### 5. Halan — merchant relationship before general contract collection

**Scope to fill:** [selected merchant offer], [product/channel], [period]. If no offer can be selected, document that gap before requesting a generic agreement.

**First request:** Please provide a dated merchant/provider document naming the legal financier for this selected offer and describing the merchant–financier relationship. Identify the applicable standard customer agreement and its document owner.

**Follow-on:** Obtain that agreement and all schedules, preserving consumer-finance and factoring roles separately where either appears.

**Why:** A register candidate and a brand product page do not establish the selected merchant relationship. The worksheet records distinct consumer-finance and factoring entries.

**Close when:** Exact passages support the selected relationship and contractual obligations. Keep insufficient evidence where the missing relationship or applicability remains unresolved.

## Shared dispatch and acceptance record

Before dispatch, record task ID, selected scope, verified official recipient role/channel, requested artifacts, known conflicting observations and proposed research use. Use the common package to request retention, reviewer access, quotation/confidentiality and reuse terms. Research receipt does not establish commercial reuse rights. Do not request customer identities, credit files, credentials or internal underwriting data for these blank-document dossiers.

After a response, preserve the original exchange and original attachments, source references, receipt time, hashes, version/effective dates and stated rights. Record each material field as observed, conflicting or unknown with page/span provenance. Obtain analyst verification of material Arabic clauses and keep scholar approval as a separate rule gate. Silence is neither agreement nor permission.

If the response is blocked or incomplete, request a source-supplied file bundle, a documented permitted channel, or owner-authorized technical access for the exact artifacts. Record unresolved gaps without substituting general brand descriptions, historical offers or arithmetic for contract clauses. Do not rotate identities or evade authentication/security controls to manufacture coverage.

The operator maintains the task record; the analyst verifies claims and scope; scholar review determines rule applicability. No response deadline or provider commitment is assumed in this proposal.
